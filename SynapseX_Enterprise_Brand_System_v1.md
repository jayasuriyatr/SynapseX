**Purpose:** Master context file for AI coding agents, designers, product teams, marketers, and future SynapseX products.

# 1. Executive Summary

SynapseX is a Neurological Intelligence Platform that performs advanced EEG analysis entirely in software using public and research-grade datasets. The platform leverages Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs), biologically inspired event-driven neural architectures, to decode brain activity for seizure detection, emotion recognition, cognitive-state estimation, and neurological research.

The SynapseX brand sits at the intersection of:

- Neuroscience
- Artificial Intelligence
- Human-Centered Computing
- Signal Processing
- Scientific Research

Every touchpoint must communicate scientific credibility, elegant engineering, biological inspiration, and trustworthy intelligence.
